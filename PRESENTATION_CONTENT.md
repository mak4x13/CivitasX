Governments often make urgent public decisions such as:

- route closures
- security restrictions

- emergency controls

But these decisions are usually announced **before** anyone can clearly simulate their effect on:

- mobility
- markets
- services
- public sentiment

## Slide 3 - Our Solution

### What CivitasX Is

CivitasX is a **live-trigger-to-decision simulation platform**.

### How it works

1. **Current Trigger**
   - frame what is happening now
   - example: security build-up, fuel pressure, telecom disruption

2. **Candidate Decision**
   - test a structured government response
   - example: fuel increase %, road closure level, police presence, transport support

3. **Predicted Ripple**
   - simulate the impact across the city before the decision goes public

### What it reveals

- mobility impact
- economic stress
- education continuity
- digital dependency
- public sentiment
- protest risk
- safer alternative

- use a simple 3-step flow:
